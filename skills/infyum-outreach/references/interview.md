# Step 2: The interview

You are a consultant at a first meeting. Everything here is **custom to this person**. Facts are your job (their Instagram account, their bio link, what is in the Sheet); only decisions are theirs.

## Layout (use exactly this for every round, no paragraphs, no intro beyond one short line)

```
❓ **Q1** - **<short title>**: <the question, one or two lines>

➡️ <your recommended answer, one line>

---

❓ **Q2** - **<short title>**: <the question>

➡️ <your recommended answer>
```

A question with no sensible default still gets a ➡️ line, for example "➡️ No default: just type it." Word each question so that "yes" accepts your recommended answer.

## Route (first, alone)

"Is this for the INFYUM team, or your own business?" Team means the shared team Sheet and the INFYUM presets. Client means you build their own Sheet.

## The 9 questions (one round, everyone gets all nine)

1. **First name and time zone.** "What's your first name (it signs the messages), and your time zone?" ➡️ No default: just type them.
2. **Who to reach.** "Who exactly do you want to reach?" ➡️ Coaches and consultants who sell programs online.
3. **Size and activity.** "What size and activity? Followers, and how recently they post." ➡️ 2,000 to 70,000 followers, best 10,000 to 30,000 first, 2 or more posts in the last 14 days. (The market is always the US; do not ask about countries.)
4. **The problem.** "What problem do you fix for them, and what on a profile shows they have it?" ➡️ No default: just type it. (For example: no booking page or funnel, a bare Linktree, "DM me" in the bio.)
5. **Company.** "Your company in one line? *INFYUM team: just reply `infyum`, we already have it.*" ➡️ No default (INFYUM team: `infyum`). On `infyum`, read `company` and `what_we_do` from the Sheet's `Settings`.
6. **Link.** "The one link I may use in messages? *INFYUM team: just reply `infyum`.*" ➡️ No default, or "none" (INFYUM team: `infyum`). On `infyum`, use the `work_sample` rows in `Settings`.
7. **Check list.** "Which exact Instagram usernames should I look for in a prospect's followers and following? If one is there, I skip that prospect." ➡️ No default: type the exact usernames (everyone types them, including the INFYUM team).
8. **Account.** "Spare or established Instagram account?" ➡️ Spare. (One honest line: automated-style activity can get any account restricted.)
9. **Today.** "How many profiles today (max 35 per Instagram account), and the full run or only part?" ➡️ 10, full run. (Part means for example find and check only, or the full run without one warm-up action such as story reactions or comments.)

## Digging in

If an answer is vague ("coaches", "small accounts"), run **one** follow-up round for the missing detail, then accept whatever you get:
- Q2: the specific niche (not just "coaches"), what they sell, one example account they would love.
- Q3: the follower range, the best band, how recently they post.
- Q4: one real example of a profile that has the problem.

## Read-back

When nothing is left to ask, read all the answers back in plain words (a short summary) and ask for a "yes" before you act on any of it.

## Remember it (there is no memory between chats, so write it down)

After the "yes", append **one** `note` line to the Activity Log whose `detail` starts with `interview:` and holds the answers in one line (niche, follower range and best band, activity rule, problem and signal, company, links choice, check-list usernames, account type). Next time, read the latest `interview:` note for this member and ask one line: "Same as last time: <summary>? Say yes, or tell me what to change." "Yes" accepts it; no ten-question interview each day. A scheduled run uses that latest note without asking.

## What the answers drive

- Q1 gives the Team row's `member`, `time_zone` and `utc_offset_hours` (work out the offset from the time zone; ask only if you cannot).
- Q2 and Q4 become your search phrases (`references/find.md`) and the niche and problem checks (`references/qualify.md`).
- Q3 sets the follower range, the order (best band first) and the activity check.
- Q5 and Q6 fill the company line and the link in the DM and the signature (`references/first-dm.md`).
- Q7 is the connected-accounts check (`references/qualify.md`, check 11).
- Q9 is today's number (never above the account's `dms_left`).
