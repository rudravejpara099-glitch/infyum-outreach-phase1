# Reference DMs (from the DM Agent Guidelines document)

*Source: the same document as `references/first-dm.md`, section "Reference examples", copied as written. These are complete outputs for real profiles. Use them only to see the expected quality, level of specificity and range of angles. **Never copy their structure, openers, phrasing or sign-offs.** Build each new DM from the new profile alone, and make sure it does not resemble these.*

## Example 1: Kami Guildner

**Profile:** Business coach for women entrepreneurs, Denver, Colorado, about 2.3K followers.

**Why this angle:** Her 12th Extraordinary Women Ignite event (Nov 5 to 7) just ended, but
the first link in her bio still points to Ignite. People finding her through the recap videos
have no next step, such as a 2027 early-bird list or waitlist. Her weekly podcast posts also
only say "link in bio."

**First DM:**
Kami, 12 years of Ignite is something else. Those Day 2 clips had real energy.
One thing I noticed though: your top bio link still points to Ignite, which has just
wrapped. Right now is probably when the most women are discovering you through
the recap videos, and there's nowhere for them to raise their hand for 2027 yet.
Is an early-bird or waitlist page already in the works? If not, I'd be glad to sketch what
that could look like for you.
Sunil | INFYUM

**Follow-up:**
Hi Kami, one more thought and then I'll leave you be 🙂 Your podcast guests (Tiffany,
Jess, Ariana) are basically Ignite's best advertisement. Each episode could lead
listeners into the 2027 waitlist instead of just a general "link in bio." Happy to map that
out on a short video if it's useful.

## Example 4: Anthony Knotts

**Profile:** Credit and funding consultant (MORE Approvals), High Point, North Carolina,
about 2.9K followers.

**Why this angle:** Almost every post tells people to DM a different keyword (MORE,
READY, APPROVED, TEAM MORE), and his only link goes straight to a Calendly. Anyone
not ready to book a call has no softer first step. His post "3 down, 21 more to go" gives a
concrete goal to build on.

**First DM:**
Anthony,"3 down, 21 to go" is a real target. Respect.
I noticed your posts use four different DM words (MORE, READY, APPROVED, TEAM
MORE), and the link in bio goes straight to a Calendly. So anyone who isn't ready to
book a call has nowhere to go, and I'd guess each DM is a hand-typed reply.
What if each keyword started its own path: a quick "approval readiness" check for
clients, a separate one for Team MORE, and the call only for people who qualify? That
could help you get to 21 without living in your DMs.
Want a 2-min Loom of how I'd set it up?
Sunil, INFYUM

**Follow-up:**
Anthony, one thing I'd test first: swap the Calendly for a short "Are you approval
ready?" quiz as your link in bio, so there's a gentler first step than a call. Happy to
mock one up for MORE Approvals if you want to see it.
